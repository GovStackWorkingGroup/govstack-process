This handbook collects the practical side of running the GovStack Technical Community: the spaces and tools GovStack provides, the processes and procedures we follow.  
This is to help the Technical Community leads, managers and facilitaors run the Technical Commnunity. The rules the Technical Community follows are in the chapter *Technical Community*. Where this handbook and that chapter differ, the chapter applies.

## Onboarding ##

All new members to the technical community should complete onboarding to be introduced to GovStack.

**Key Components:**
- Join Technical Community Form *link*. 
- Technical Community Registry *to do*. 
- Initial onboarding response email *link*. 
- Onboarding slide deck *link*. 
- Post onboarding template *link*. 


**Process** (TO DO: MOVE TO MERMAID)
1. Individual applies to community using the "Join Technical Community Form"
2. Community Manager logs application (and date of application) 
3. Community Manager responds with proforma email (LINK) 
4. Community Manager sets up Monthly Onboarding calls (am/pm)
5. Community Manager invites all who have applied in the last 3 months that hasnt attended an onboarding call to the meetings.
6.  Onboarding calls run using slide deck.
7. Attendees are added to the Technical Community Register.
8.  1 week post call Community Manager sends individual emails to each attendee thats contains the following
  - Welcome to Technical Community
  - Key Links to assets
  - Reminder of groups they registered and interest in
  - Details of WGs and Meeting times
  - Offer to assist in introduction to a WG
  - Offer for call or support when needed.

9. Followups are done a week later if no response.
