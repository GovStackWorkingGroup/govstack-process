title: Technical Community Handbook
description: Practical information for running the GovStack Technical Community.
---

This handbook collects the practical side of running the GovStack Technical Community: the spaces and tools GovStack
provides, the processes and procedures we follow.  This is to help the Technical Community leads, managers and facilitaors run the Technical Commnunity. The rules the Technical Community follows are in the chapter *Technical Community*. Where this handbook and that chapter differ, the
chapter applies.
