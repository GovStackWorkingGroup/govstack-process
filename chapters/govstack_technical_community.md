## What the Technical Community is

The Technical Community is the community of experts with an interest in governmental interoperability regardless of areas of expertise or area of practice within GovStack. You remain in the Technical Community as long as you remain within GovStack regardless of whether what Working Group, Country Engagements or Freelance Engagement you may practice in at any time.
Membership is through application and onboarding.  This is a one-time activity.

The Community provides an **ongoing space** to belong share ideas with fellow community members, nuture and grow in GovStack and receive updates on other work throughout the journey of a member in GovStack. 

## Membership ##

Membership is confered after application and onboarding and is enduring regardless of what activities/roles/practices a member is undertaking whilst they stay active within GovStack.

Membership is only removed on the following basis:

1. When requested by the member.
2. When they are no longer active (after a period of significant inactivity) within GovStack.
3. Following due process relating to GovStack Code of Conduct Breaches.

Members **MUST**:
Have submitted an **application**
Attended an **onboarding call**
Agreed to and abide by the **GovStack Code of Conduct**

## Activities of the Technical Community ##

Its members convene and communicate through a number of methods:
