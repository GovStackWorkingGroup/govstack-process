---
title: GovStack Namespaces
---

The purpose of GovStack Namespaces is to provide the semantics for maintaining and serving stable identifiers for referenceable objects in GovStack. The following namespaces are currently maintained:

| Object type | Scope | Available namespaces | URI |
|---|---|---|---|
|GovStack Core documents|Publication series of core govstack documents|govstack:core:||
|GovStack Cross-Functional Requirements Specifications|Publication series containing machine-readable requirements|govstack:specs:cfr:|
|GovStack Building Block Specifications|Base namespace for Building Block specifications|govstack:specs:bb:|
|GovStack Working Groups|Base namespace of GovStack Working Group Objects|govstack:wg:|
|GovStack Guides|Publication series about non-normative guides|govstack:guide:|
|GovStack Shared Terminologies|Base namespace for shared terminology|govstack:terminology:|
