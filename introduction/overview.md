This document aims to cover all aspects of GovStack Publication lifecycle, including the operating procedures for the Working Groups that create specifications. It’s objective is to ensure there are clear processes for the different participants and stakeholders using, building and implementing the GovStack framework.

This document is part of the Core series of GovStack Publications. Other documents in the series that will allow readers to work with the GovStack approach are as follows:

- [GovStack Architecture](https://specs.govstack.global/architecture): Defines the structural and technical constraints and decisions that shape a system's overall design to achieve technical interoperability: the ability for different autonomous systems and Building Blocks to collaborate and provide an ecosystem of value. This document lays the foundation of what GovStack is for.
- GovStack Specification Framework: Defines how to write specifications requirements, which are the basis for implementing Building Blocks.
- GovStack Terminologies: Defines terminology writing and usage in GovStack.

This document deals with the following topics:

- GovStack namespaces to identify publications and working groups
- Types of GovStack Publication series and their namespaces
- The publication lifecycle
- Compiling and publishing govstack specifications
- GovStack Working Groups, their namespaces, roles and processes
